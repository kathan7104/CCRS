029: Introduction
030: The Animal Farming Goods Management System project, we have created a Water Analysis module in odoo.  This module enables the creation of CRM leads based on water analysis data. It also features functionality to  generate meeting rooms and send emails to the admin, users, and attendees. Then, we have to create a product  from the product portfolio. In the product portfolio, first, when we create a product, we update to testing, then  create a product. Then, we create a sale-to-purchase flow and generate a receipt from the purchase order. Then,  we log in with the product manager ID. The admin has set the rights in the product manager to set only those  records which is assigned to the product manager in the water analysis document. Then we have set a filter in  water analysis for view to only which document status is done, draft and group by filter.

047: Project scope
048: 1. Goods Sales and Purchasing:
049: ● The system will manage the sale of farming goods such as animal feed, health supplies, and equipment. It  will also handle purchasing necessary products to maintain the farm's operations.
050: ● Bill of Materials (BoM):
051: ○ A comprehensive Bill of Materials (BoM) will be generated for each purchase, listing all necessary  items and quantities required for farming operations. This helps track inventory, manage stock  levels, and optimize purchasing decisions.
052: 2. Water Analysis System:
053: ● Created a custom water analysis module with three security groups: Representative (own documents  only), User (all documents), and Administrator (full access).
054: ● Created water analysis records based on customer requirements.
055: ● "Send" button to send emails or log messages.
056: ● When send button press and stage to "Done" if attachments exist or "Error" if missing. ● Then generate lead and create a meeting room.
057: ● Integrated with the Documents app by creating a "Water Analysis", attachments are moved there and  accessible via a smart button on the customer record.
058: 3. Meeting Management:
059: ● Attendee Invitations and Email Validation:
060: ○ The system will send email invitations to the selected meeting attendees. Each attendee will  confirm their participation or reject the invitation via email, and the system will track these  responses.
061: ● Tracking Meeting Outcome:
062: ○ Once the meeting has taken place, the system will evaluate the discussion's outcome. Based on the  decisions made and actions taken during the meeting, the meeting will be marked as either  Successful or Unsuccessful.
063: 4. Lead Generation in the CRM Module:
064: ● Lead Generation from Meetings:
065: ○ The system will generate leads based on the discussions and decisions made during meetings. For  example, if a solution is proposed for improving water quality or buying more supplies, a lead will  be created in the CRM module for further follow-up and sales.
066: ● CRM Integration:
067: ○ The CRM system will track all leads generated, including details such as contact information,  status of discussions, and potential sales opportunities.
068: Page: 9
069: 5. Lead Validation and Success Tracking:
070: ● Meeting Outcome Determines Lead Status:
071: ○ After the meeting, the lead will be either Won or Lost based on whether the meeting led to  actionable decisions and successful outcomes.
072: ● Labeling in CRM:
073: ○ The CRM system automatically assigns labels like Won or Lost to each lead depending on the  meeting's success or failure. A "Won" lead would indicate that the proposed solution was accepted,  while a "Lost" lead would indicate that the proposal was rejected or no agreement was reached.

125: 1. Module Integration
126: ● Existing systems manage operations in separate modules with limited data sharing, while our system  fully integrates CRM, inventory, water analysis, and meetings into a unified platform.
127: 2. Water Analysis
128: ● Water analysis tracking and meeting generating is available only in specialized tools and is isolated  from operations, whereas our system embeds water analysis into the operational flow and triggers  meetings based on real-time data.
129: 3. Automated Meeting Scheduling
130: ● Meetings in current platforms are scheduled manually without system context, but our solution  automates meeting creation when critical conditions like customer request for water analysis.
131: 4. Email Response Tracking
132: ● Most platforms send static meeting invites without tracking participation, while ours tracks email  responses in real time and confirms attendance automatically.
133: 5. Lead Generation from Operations
134: ● CRMs in existing systems rely on manual input for lead creation, whereas our system auto generates leads from meetings and tags them as Won or Lost based on real outcomes.
135: 6. Traceability and Compliance
136: ● Other systems provide partial traceability in specific modules, but our system ensures complete  traceability across goods, meetings, reports, and lead outcomes for full regulatory compliance.
137: 7. Technology Stack and Flexibility

143: ● Functional Requirements:
144: 1. User Management
145: ● The system should allow the creation, modification, and deletion of user accounts with  different roles (e.g., product manager, veterinarians, suppliers).

147: ● The system should track the inventory levels of farming goods and notify users when  stock is low.
148: ● Users should be able to create purchase orders for products and generate a Bill of  Materials (BoM) for each purchase.
149: ● The system should handle supplier information and automatically generate reorder  suggestions based on inventory levels.
150: 3. Water Analysis
151: ● The system provides an integration with Meeting Rooms and CRM, to provide a smooth  interface to users to generate meetings.

153: ● Once the Water analysis is confirmed and is in done state, it automatically generates lead  in the CRM.
154: 4. Meeting Management
155: ● The system should allow the creation and scheduling of meetings, Invitations should be  sent automatically to salesperson and attendees and their responses should be tracked. ● Post-meeting, the system should allow for the tracking of outcomes and actions, with  status indicators for each meeting (e.g., "Successful" or "Unsuccessful").
156: 5. Lead Generation and CRM Integration
157: ● The system should automatically generate leads based on discussions and decisions made  during meetings.
158: ● Each lead should be linked to a customer profile and tracked in the CRM system,  including status updates (e.g., "Won," "Lost").
159: ● The CRM system should allow users to monitor the progress of leads, including follow up actions and potential sales opportunities.
160: 6. Reporting
